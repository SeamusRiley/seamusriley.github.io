# Chromatic Atlas of Art — Artwork Database
# Format: HUE | SAT | BRI | Title | Creator | Type
#
# HUE: 0–360  (theme axis, wraps around)
#   0   = Survival & Violence (red)
#   30  = Desire & Sex (orange)
#   60  = Power & Ambition (yellow)
#   90  = Growth & Becoming (lime)
#   120 = Nature & Exploration (green)
#   150 = Wonder & the Uncanny (teal)
#   180 = Grief & Loss (cyan)
#   210 = Solitude & Loneliness (sky)
#   240 = Justice & Society (blue)
#   270 = Memory & Identity (indigo)
#   300 = Love & Devotion (purple)
#   330 = Fate & Transcendence (magenta)
#
# SAT: 0–100  (intensity axis)
#   0   = slice of life / no dramatic intensity
#   100 = maximum intensity (Godzilla, opera, extreme drama)
#
# BRI: 0–100  (realism axis)
#   0   = stark documentary realism (nearly black)
#   100 = pure myth / legend / fable (nearly white)
#
# Lines beginning with # are comments and are ignored.
