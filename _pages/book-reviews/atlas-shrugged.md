### Review

I love this book like my life itself. Its heroes live in every moment of my days, reminding me that there is no need for pain, or fear, or guilt, and my proper existence is pride, joy, and motive power. As a clear, intricate, brilliant piece of psychological literature it is an unparalleled masterpiece.
