My favorite litmus test for political philosophers is whether they have a theory of politics and ethics that actually integrates women into society.

Indeed there are fewer women philosophers, entrepreneurs, lawyers, etc. Indeed there probably always will be. The question is: do you want to live in a society that cares?

It is *incredibly* disappointing how many tacitly concede that women are subrational and therefore have to either be given a handicap, or worse, be regarded as cattle. A good political theory allows for differences between men and women but makes no law or excuse regarding them as different classes. "Boys will be boys" and all derisive gendered talk has to be torn out by the root. People need the confidence to set just laws and let all gendered gaps such as wage gaps fall where they may, rather than trying to force equal (or unequal) outcomes through regulation or social pressure.

I support all bathrooms and all sports being unisex just as we now have coeducation and all-gender workplaces. If we are concerned about girls being violated in bathrooms, are we not also concerned about this happening to boys? If men dominate women in all contact sports, why do we not simply turn our attention to sports like gymnastics that score on grace rather than brute force? Once upon a time women's physical weakness and men's lack of intelligence meant that women had to be kept as fragile property and protected in all areas. Those times are gone, but people have been slow to catch up.
